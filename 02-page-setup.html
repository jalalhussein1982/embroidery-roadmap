Section HPosting system

The asset set — five pieces per project

  1. Process reel — 10–15 s, original or trending audio. Not licensed catalogue tracks: they limit reach and can be muted.
  2. Reveal reel — 5–8 s, hook in the first second: the finished piece, then the stitch.
  3. Carousel — piece in situ (on the wall, on the table) → detail → packaging → “how to order” slide.
  4. Story series across three days.
  5. Client reaction / tagged photo when it arrives.
