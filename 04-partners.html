Partner Prospecting Pipeline

Build a list of Prague businesses that meet gift-givers before Anna does, score them, approach them by hand, and turn 4–6 of them into ongoing referral sources.

The one rule

This is a list of partners, not customers. Nothing in this pipeline sends an automated message — ever.

60–120businesses collected and scored
40actually contacted (Tier A + B)
8–12realistic replies
4–6active partnerships — the goal

Step 4Outreach protocol — by hand

  1. Days 1–7 · Warm upFollow, like 3–5 posts, leave 2 real comments. Do this before any message. It is the difference between a 5% reply rate and 25%.
  2. Day 8 · ContactOne message. DM if they are active on Instagram, email if the site lists one. Maximum 5 new contacts per day. Personalise the first line with something specific from their feed.
  3. Day 18 · Follow upOne short follow-up. Then stop.
  4. On reply · SampleTier A, or proactively for the top 5: hand-deliver in Prague, photograph the handover, post about it and tag them. This is content regardless of the outcome.
  5. Weekly · ReviewUpdate status in the sheet. Count replies, samples out, tags received.

GuardrailsAnti-patterns — do not

  • Do not DM individuals found via hashtags or a competitor’s follower list.
  • Do not use any auto-follow, auto-like or auto-DM tool. Blocks start around 20–50 actions per hour on a small account.
  • Do not send more than 5 first-contact messages a day, or the same template unchanged.
  • Do not collect or store data on private individuals — see file 05, GDPR.
