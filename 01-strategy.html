The one-sentence diagnosis

Anna is broadcasting to the wrong crowd: 86 of her 92 followers are friends or other embroiderers. The job is not “get more followers”. The job is get the right 500 people to see the page, and make it easy for them to order.

Who buysIt is never the baby, and never the couple

It is the gift-giver: a grandmother, a godparent, a bridesmaid, a friend. They have a deadline — a birth, a wedding, a birthday — and one worry:

“I want to bring something nobody else will bring.”

Every word on the page should speak to that person.

The partner logicWhy a list of businesses, not a list of people

What not to do

Do not build a list of pregnant women and engaged couples and message them one by one. Instagram will block the account; EU privacy law forbids collecting individuals’ data for marketing without a legal basis; and cold messages to strangers about a gift do not convert anyway.

Think of it like this. A newborn photographer in Prague photographs 15 babies a month. Each of those families has grandparents and friends looking for a gift right now. If the photographer uses Anna’s hoop as a prop and tags her, Anna is shown to all 15 families and their gift-givers — with the photographer’s implicit endorsement.

One partner = a stream of warm buyers, every month, for free.

The list is a list of partners to approach, not customers to sell to. For each one:

  1. Warm upFollow them, like and comment on a few posts over a week, so the name is familiar.
  2. One messageWritten by hand, five per day maximum, offering a free sample piece in exchange for using it as a prop, displaying it, or tagging her.
  3. Send the samplePhotograph the handover. Post about the collaboration — it is content regardless of the outcome.
  4. Track and stopLog replies in the sheet. Follow up once after ten days. Then stop.
The arithmetic

50 partner targets → realistically 8–12 replies → 4–6 active partnerships. That is enough to change the account’s audience within two months.

Apify is fine for collecting the business profiles — public business accounts, never private individuals. Not for sending anything. → File 04 has the target types, the scoring, the Apify workflow and the message templates.

Free contentThe customers she already has

Around 20 pieces have been delivered. Zero customers have ever tagged the page. Each of those hoops is on a nursery wall being photographed weekly.

Every piece now leaves with a small card — handle, QR code, “tag me when it’s on the wall”, and a discount code for the buyer’s next occasion. Message the past 20 clients once, personally, asking for a photo and a tag. This costs nothing and is the fastest source of both content and social proof.

Before anything elseCzech legal and tax

Selling regularly on Instagram without a trade licence is illegal in Czechia, and personalised goods have specific consumer-law rules. Nothing dramatic — but it must be set up before the page starts pulling real orders.
